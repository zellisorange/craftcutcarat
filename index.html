The person behind the bench

Our Story.

Why Craft Cut Carat exists — and why it matters.

🔥
The Obsession

I'm Ellis St. Croix — a self-taught lapidary based in Cambridge, Ontario. I cut on an Ultra Tec VL Classic, the gold standard of faceting machines. Every stone that leaves my bench has been touched by my hands, cut to my standards, and polished to a mirror finish I'm proud of.

⚙️
The Background

I'm a multi-disciplined artist — surrealistic acrylic paintings, precision steel fabrication, electroplating, foundry smelting and core-making skills, welding, and a background in classical music. Every skill I've built over a lifetime demanded the same thing: patience, precision, and an obsession with detail. That path led me exactly here — to gemstone faceting and stone setting, where art and science meet at the tip of a lap. Every facet I cut carries all of it.

💎
The Stones

Sapphire, ruby, topaz, tanzanite, fluorite, apatite, kyanite and more. Lab grown and natural rough sourced for quality. I cut precision faceted gemstones and craft finished jewellery. Every piece leaves my bench cut to my standards and polished to a mirror finish I'm proud of.

🇨🇦
Made in Canada

Every stone is cut right here in Cambridge, Ontario. When you buy from Craft Cut Carat you're buying directly from the person who cut it — no middleman, no mystery. Just a cutter who loves what he does and puts that love into every stone.
